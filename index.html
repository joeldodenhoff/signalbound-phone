Connect for live diagnostics. Matching firmware also records Diagnostic Check on the SD card.

Download diagnostic-current.log and diagnostic-previous.log from the board's web file list, or copy them from the SD card. Import either here to recover samples from a Bluetooth interruption. Local logs rotate at roughly 1 MB each.
